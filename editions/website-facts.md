# Edition facts for the website

Inputs for the website copy. Everything here is backed by a page in this
folder; if a claim is not listed, do not publish it.

## May be said

- Desktop "Build your world": layout, theme and experience mode are chosen
  independently; Supported.
- Studio "Make something real": a reversible PipeWire Low Latency profile
  (`horneroctl studio audio apply|revert|status`) with live xrun and DSP-load
  readout, Studio diagnostics, allow-listed render jobs, Studio mode; Supported.
- Gaming "Play on your terms": a mode that holds notifications, pauses
  polling, hides the desktop layer, inhibits idle and turns motion and glow
  off under a fullscreen window on Hyprland; a doctor that checks Vulkan,
  Proton prerequisites, GameMode, optional Gamescope/MangoHud; a Steam
  launch-options printer; Supported, handheld session Experimental.
- The three editions are one platform: edition x mode x layout x theme.

## Must not be said

- Any frame rate, latency, "lowest latency" or performance claim.
- Any game (including Cyberpunk 2077) or application "works" or "is
  certified"; any hardware is "supported" or "qualified".
- That REAPER, Ardour, Guitarix, OBS, Blender or Kdenlive workflows are
  validated; they are installable packages only.
- That Gamescope/MangoHud launch compositions were tested on hardware.
- That Gaming mode works on Niri (fullscreen detection is Hyprland-only).

## Maturity labels

Desktop, Studio, Gaming (Supported); Gamescope handheld session
and VR Experimental; Wine/Lutris/Bottles Planned.
